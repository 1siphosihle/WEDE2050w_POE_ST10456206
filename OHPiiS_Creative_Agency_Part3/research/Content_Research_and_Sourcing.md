# OHPiiS Creative Agency — Content Research & Sourcing

## Organisation research
OHPiiS Creative Agency is the approved hypothetical target organisation for the web-development project. The website content has been written as original project content rather than copied from an external organisation.

## Target audience research
The proposed audience consists of artists, fashion/lifestyle brands, entrepreneurs, small businesses, organisations and creative professionals who need visual identity, digital design, content production or creative direction.

## Page content
- **Home:** agency introduction, positioning statement, call to action and service overview.
- **About:** story, approach, mission and vision.
- **Services:** six service categories with descriptions.
- **Enquiry:** project brief form and budget range.
- **Contact:** contact details, two locations and contact form.

## Part 2 design research
MDN Web Docs was used as a technical reference for Flexbox, CSS Grid, responsive design and media queries. W3C accessibility guidance informed focus states and general accessible design considerations. GitHub documentation informed repository/README practices.

## Assets
The abstract studio graphics in the `images/` folder were generated specifically for this student project and are therefore original project assets. No external photographic asset is used in the Part 2 build.
